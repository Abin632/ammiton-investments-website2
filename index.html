Company Profile

Who We Are

Established in Nairobi, Kenya, in 2012, Ammiton Investments has grown from its initial focus as a stationery retail point to become a leading and diversified supplier of a wide spectrum of office, technology and institutional solutions.

Today the company serves as a key resource for businesses and institutions seeking everything from essential stationery and computing equipment to uniforms, workwear, office furniture and general supplies.

Our Mission

To deliver increasing value and quality by fostering long-term and mutually beneficial relationships through:

  • Solutions tailored to customers’ specific needs
  • High-quality products at competitive prices
  • Unparalleled execution in all interactions and services
